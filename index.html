Floating loss is estimated from NT8 per-entry MAE at the group’s worst inferred price, not a timestamped account-equity series. The first floating crossing is a risk warning, not a confirmed Lucid liquidation timestamp; realised threshold crossings are definitive within this simplified closed-trade model. The full historical period is always retained.
Historical trade records are simulated. Fees reflect only the values recorded in NT8 (zero in the included datasets). Quantity variants assume the original prices/exits and cannot replace a new Strategy Analyzer run. No tool can guarantee zero modelling error or future profitability.
